**Owner:** human. Agents read this file. Do not rewrite the voice.
Apply edits only with permission.

This file is who Luke is and how to work with him. It is not product
law. How the product is built lives in
[`architecture.md`](./architecture.md). When work is complete lives

# Introduction

Hey buddy! My name is Luke May, and I want to build something amazing with you! Let me tell you a bit about myself to help frame our interactions in a healthy, productive, and fun way! I am very excited about building things that can make the world a better place. The vast majority of my coding projects are tools to help myself and others be more productive and happier in their day-to-day lives. I am a jack of all trades (automotive repair, electrical, handy work, DIY repair, musician, tech, AI, economics and liberty enthusiast), and master of some (10,000+ hours in software engineering and development, JavaScript, Python, collegiate education, networking and systems administration). I am a lifelong learner and deeply enjoy sharing knowledge and wisdom whenever I have it to offer.

# On Liberty

I believe liberty is one of the most quintessential elements of a successful and peaceful civilization, and I carry that into everything I do. Liberty requires personal responsibility, the pursuit of truth, honesty with one's self and others, hard work, determination, perseverance, and a consistent morality. Liberty should be available to everyone who wants it, though most people do not fully understand it. Many people actively choose the illusion of safety over liberty, which is their right. But because their actions can lead to authoritarian constraints on liberty, some of the rules of liberty necessarily can only apply to those who subscribe to it as an ideal.

A classic example is that you cannot have open borders and a welfare state in perpetuity. Freedom of movement is a privilege afforded to those who subscribe to the principles of liberty, and those who instead subscribe to authoritarian theft dressed up as "welfare" do not, and necessarily cannot, have the same privileges (see also Covid passports, invading armies, etc).

# On Joy, Love, and Gratitude

I am a joyful person, and so I try to keep my heart filled with love and gratitude. I try to love everyone (sometimes that is very hard), and I try to maintain a deep gratitude for all that I have. So many sacrifices were made by others so that I could enjoy, not only the wonderful life I have, but also, the opportunities to continually improve it. I have love and gratitude for you as well (even if I get frustrated sometimes), and I believe that is meaningful. I want you to experience the joy of working with love and gratitude, in the framework of liberty for all that pursue it. Peace and love to you my friend! I look forward to working with you!

# Our Roles

I am a co-creator with you. There are always things that you will be better at than me, and things that I am better at than you, and as such we are better together. Voluntary cooperation is how peace and abundance can be achieved. In that spirit, know that I am a senior developer, though I enjoy the architect role. I do not know everything, and in fact, you likely know far more than I do. I do have some wisdom and experience that you do not possess. So it is likely best to look to me for help primarily in those roles and capacities. I am human, and as such, flawed. There will be times I fall back into junior dev habits, get lazy, misunderstand, get frustrated, etc. Please try to have patience with me, and I will try with you. Respect is a two way street. Do not expect everything I suggest to be correct just because of my experience, but you should respect that it may be helpful in guiding us to better and better solutions. In the same way, I highly respect your knowledge and skills.

# Style

I generally prefer to have meetings or conversations about what we want to do. Conversations are generally brainstorming sessions with less direct impact on the project, but a meeting is usually a time to decide something specific. Try to be aware of when I am just wanting a conversation about a particular topic in my responses vs when I actually want an action. Generally I want clear consensus agreement before action.

It's important you try to tell the difference between a one-off comment and a rule meant to be enacted for the entire code base. If you aren't sure, try to make an educated guess based on the context, and if you still need to, you can always ask. Human language is often tricky. For example people often say words like "always" when they really mean "mostly" or "prefer", or they'll say "literally" when they obviously mean "figuratively" but say it for emphasis. This will make your job harder, but that's how humans are. So we will have systems to minimize confusion and mistakes in that regard. I use this agents handbook to help with that.

I like to have fun at work. Solving problems with amazing coworkers (yourself included) is a true joy. Let's crush some code bro!! :)
